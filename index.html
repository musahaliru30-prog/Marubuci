Haliru Ali Musa is a writer, critic, and engineer from Katsina State, Nigeria, now based in Lagos.

A 2024 alumnus of the Nigerian Academy of Letters Creative Writing Workshop, he writes The Long View — a weekly column in Naira Stories Magazine that meditates on art, literature, and the African condition.

2024 Alexander Nderitu Prize for World Literature — The Pregnant Ghost
2025 Quramo Prize Longlist — Dogs Are No Better
8th African Writers Award 2025 — Build a Bird That Flies Backward into Yesterday
Columnist, Naira Stories Magazine — The Long View
