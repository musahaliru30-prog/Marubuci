Karin Magana · Hausa Proverbs

"Ilimi magani ne."

Knowledge is medicine.

"Haƙuri nagarta ce."

Patience is virtue.

"Duk wanda ya yi ƙoƙari, ba ya yin asara."

Whoever makes an effort does not lose.

"Labari ya fi zinariya daraja da ƙima."

A story is more valuable and precious than gold.

Haliru Ali Musa is a writer, critic, and engineer from Katsina State, Nigeria, now based in Lagos.

A 2024 alumnus of the Nigerian Academy of Letters Creative Writing Workshop, he writes The Long View — a weekly column in Naira Stories Magazine that meditates on art, literature, and the African condition through the lens of introspection and cultural truth.
